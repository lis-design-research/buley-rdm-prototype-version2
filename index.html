Data Management Plans and funder requirements

A DMP explains how data will be organized, documented, protected, shared, and preserved.

Writing a plan and implementing it are different tasks.

*Pro Tip: review funder specifications as you prepare your proposal to capture requirements and cost of compliance early!

Prepare your plan

  1. Identify the funder and review the current funding announcement.
  2. Describe data types, formats, and documentation needs.
  3. Plan storage, backup, access, security, and privacy.
  4. Consider sharing restrictions, repository options, and preservation.
  5. Draft your DMP and review it as the project develops.

Research data lifecycle

Use the stage relevant to your work. These activities overlap and may require different expertise.

1. Plan

Research requirements, responsibilities, DMP, and data-sharing expectations.

2. Collect and store

Appropriate storage, backup, permissions, and sensitive data.

3. Organize and document

File naming, version control, README files, and metadata.

4. Analyze and maintain

Record methods, transformations, software, and changes.

5. Share and preserve

Consent, licensing, repositories, retention, and reuse.

6. Close out and hand over

Document decisions, locations, access, and responsibilities when personnel change.
